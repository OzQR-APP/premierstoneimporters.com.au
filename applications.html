Kitchen Benchtops

The kitchen is the heart of the home, and nothing elevates it quite like a stunning stone benchtop. Whether you prefer the timeless elegance of granite, the luxurious veining of marble, or the durability of engineered quartz, we have the perfect stone for your kitchen.

Recommended Stones:

  • Granite: Extremely durable, heat-resistant, and available in stunning natural patterns
  • Quartz: Non-porous, low maintenance, consistent patterns and colours
  • Quartzite: Natural stone with exceptional hardness and unique veining
  • Marble: Luxurious appearance, ideal for statement kitchens (requires sealing)

Wall Cladding

Natural stone cladding adds texture, depth, and visual interest to both interior and exterior walls. From dramatic feature walls to sophisticated facades, stone cladding creates a lasting impression.

Popular Applications:

  • Living room feature walls
  • Fireplace surrounds
  • Kitchen splashbacks
  • External building facades
  • Commercial reception areas

Recommended Stones:

  • Travertine: Classic texture with natural variation
  • Limestone: Elegant, soft tones for sophisticated spaces
  • Granite: Durable option for high-traffic areas

Stone Flooring

Stone flooring is an investment that adds lasting value and beauty to any space. Natural stone floors are incredibly durable, easy to maintain, and each piece is unique, creating one-of-a-kind interiors.

Benefits of Stone Flooring:

  • Exceptional durability - lasts a lifetime with proper care
  • Natural temperature regulation - cool in summer
  • Hypoallergenic - doesn't harbour dust or allergens
  • Timeless aesthetic that never goes out of style
  • Increases property value

Recommended Stones:

  • Travertine: Perfect for Mediterranean and contemporary styles
  • Limestone: Soft, warm tones ideal for living areas
  • Granite: Excellent for high-traffic commercial spaces
Browse Flooring Stones
