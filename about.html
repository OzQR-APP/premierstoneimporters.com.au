About Us

Your trusted partner in premium natural stone

Home / About Us

Australia's Trusted Stone Supplier

Premier Stone Importers has been serving Australian homeowners, builders, architects, and stonemasons with the finest quality natural and engineered stone. Based in Melbourne, we've built our reputation on exceptional quality, competitive pricing, and outstanding customer service.

We source our stone directly from the world's most renowned quarries in Brazil, India, Italy, Spain, and beyond. This direct relationship with suppliers allows us to offer an extensive selection at competitive prices while maintaining the highest quality standards.

Whether you're renovating a kitchen, designing a luxury bathroom, creating a stunning feature wall, or completing a commercial project, our experienced team is here to guide you through the selection process and ensure you find the perfect stone for your vision.

What sets us apart from other stone suppliers

Quality First

Every slab is carefully inspected before it enters our inventory. We only stock stone that meets our exacting standards for colour consistency, structural integrity, and finish quality.

Who We Serve

Homeowners: Whether you're building your dream home or renovating an existing space, our team helps you understand the characteristics of different stones and find the perfect match for your lifestyle and aesthetic. We'll send you photos, videos, and samples to help you make the right choice.

Stonemasons & Fabricators: We're a reliable partner for trade professionals, offering consistent supply, competitive trade pricing, and a wide selection to meet diverse project requirements.

Builders & Developers: From single homes to multi-unit developments, we supply stone at scale with the reliability and quality consistency your projects demand.

Architects & Designers: Specify with confidence knowing we stock unique, high-quality materials that will bring your creative vision to life.

How We Work

Simple process, exceptional results

Getting the perfect stone for your project is easy. Simply reach out via WhatsApp or email with your requirements, and our experienced team will guide you through our extensive collection.

We'll send you photos and videos of available slabs, provide samples if needed, and answer all your questions about stone characteristics, care, and maintenance.

Our Process

  • 1. Discuss: Tell us about your project and preferences
  • 2. Explore: We'll share options that match your needs
  • 3. Select: Choose your perfect slab from photos/videos
  • 4. Deliver: Australia-wide delivery to your door
