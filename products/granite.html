About Granite

Granite is an igneous rock formed from cooled magma, making it one of the hardest and most durable natural stones available. Each slab features unique patterns created by different mineral compositions, ensuring no two surfaces are exactly alike.

Why Choose Granite?

  • Exceptional Durability: Resistant to scratches, chips, and cracks
  • Heat Resistant: Won't scorch or burn from hot pans
  • Unique Beauty: Natural patterns never repeat
  • Long-lasting: With proper sealing, lasts a lifetime
  • Value: Adds significant value to your property

Granite Care Guide

Keep your granite looking beautiful for decades

🧽

Daily Cleaning

Clean with warm water and mild dish soap. Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive cleaners that can damage the sealant.

🛡️

Sealing

Reseal your granite every 1-2 years to maintain stain resistance. We can recommend quality sealers.

⚠️

Avoid Damage

While durable, avoid dropping heavy objects on edges. Use cutting boards rather than cutting directly on the surface.
