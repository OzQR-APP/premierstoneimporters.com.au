About Quartz

Engineered quartz combines approximately 93% natural quartz crystals with polymer resins and pigments, creating a surface that offers the beauty of natural stone with enhanced durability and consistency.

Why Choose Quartz?

  • Non-Porous: Resists stains, bacteria, and doesn't require sealing
  • Consistent Patterns: Reliable colour and pattern across slabs
  • Low Maintenance: Easy to clean with just soap and water
  • Highly Durable: Scratch and chip resistant
  • Wide Range: Available in colours and patterns not found in nature

Quartz Care Guide

Simple maintenance for lasting beauty

🧽

Easy Cleaning

Simply wipe with warm water and mild detergent. No special cleaners required for everyday maintenance.

🚫

No Sealing Required

Unlike natural stone, quartz never needs sealing. The non-porous surface is permanently protected.

🔥

Heat Caution

While durable, use trivets for hot pans. Extreme heat can damage the resin in quartz surfaces.
