About Travertine

Travertine is a natural limestone formed by mineral deposits from hot springs. Used since ancient Roman times, it brings warmth and character to any space with its distinctive porous texture and earthy tones.

Why Choose Travertine?

  • Timeless Appeal: Classic beauty that never dates
  • Warm Tones: Earthy colours complement any décor
  • Multiple Finishes: Polished, honed, tumbled, or brushed
  • Indoor/Outdoor: Suitable for pools, patios, and interiors
  • Natural Texture: Distinctive character with natural pitting
