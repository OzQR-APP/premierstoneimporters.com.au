Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer samples?

Yes, we can provide small samples for most of our stones. Contact us with your requirements and we'll arrange samples to be sent to you.

Do you provide installation services?

We specialise in supplying stone rather than installation. However, we can recommend experienced stonemasons and fabricators in your area.

What areas do you deliver to?

We deliver Australia-wide. Delivery costs vary depending on location and order size. Contact us for a delivery quote.

Can I select my specific slab?

Absolutely! We can send you photos and videos of available slabs so you can choose the perfect one. Each natural stone slab is unique.
